Mission & Vision

Our maison d'hôte is a charming eco-friendly guesthouse, located in the serene and picturesque town of Tabarka, Tunisia. Nestled in a peaceful area, it has been lovingly restored with great care and attention to detail to preserve its original architecture and charm. The guesthouse features 5 unique and elegantly decorated bedrooms, each blending traditional Tunisian style with modern eco-conscious touches. Guests can enjoy a beautiful courtyard, a hammam, a pool, a sunbathing area, and a lovely garden. Our commitment to sustainability ensures a relaxing and environmentally friendly stay.
