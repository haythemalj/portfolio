About the project

Visinova is a smart-glasses concept exploring tools such as obstacle awareness, text reading, and object recognition to support everyday mobility.

What this demo does

The controls switch between preset examples in your browser. No images or camera data are captured, uploaded, or analyzed; there is no connected device or live inference service.
