Photo manipulation

Shoe House

A surreal composite built in Photoshop: a sneaker turned into a house, set in an alpine meadow at sunset, with a child walking the boardwalk towards its door.

The work is in the blending: matching light direction and colour temperature across the source photos, painting the cast shadow, and adding depth haze so the pieces read as one scene.
